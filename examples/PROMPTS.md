# Example prompts

Prompts that work well with SDXL and LCM-LoRA. Try them as-is, then change one thing at a time: the subject, the setting, or the seed.

Settings used unless noted otherwise: 4 steps, guidance 1.0, 1024 x 1024.

## Landscapes and nature

* "A cozy cabin in a snowy forest at dusk, warm lights in the windows, cinematic lighting"
* "A misty mountain valley at sunrise, a small lake reflecting the peaks, photorealistic"
* "Cherry blossom trees lining a quiet street in Kyoto, petals falling, soft morning light"
* "A lighthouse on a rocky cliff during a storm, dramatic waves, dark moody sky"

## Cities and architecture

* "Futuristic city skyline at sunset, flying vehicles, neon signs, ultra detailed"
* "Isometric illustration of a tiny coffee shop on a floating island, soft pastel colors"
* "A medieval castle on a hill above a village, golden hour, epic wide shot"
* "Cyberpunk alley in the rain, neon reflections on wet pavement, cinematic"

## People and portraits

* "Portrait of an astronaut standing on a neon lit alien planet, dramatic rim light"
* "Studio portrait of a woman with curly hair, soft window light, shallow depth of field"
* "An old fisherman mending a net on a wooden boat, warm sunset light, photorealistic"

## Animals

* "A red panda sitting on a mossy branch in a bamboo forest, soft sunlight, detailed fur"
* "A majestic snowy owl in flight against a full moon, night sky, sharp feathers"
* "A golden retriever puppy playing in autumn leaves, joyful, warm light"

## Food and product

* "A bowl of ramen with steam rising, chopsticks resting on the rim, cozy restaurant, close up"
* "A minimalist perfume bottle on a marble surface, soft shadows, luxury product photography"
* "A stack of pancakes with berries and maple syrup, morning light, overhead shot"

## Art styles

* "A watercolor painting of a coastal town, loose brushstrokes, soft colors"
* "Pixel art of a small village at night, glowing windows, starry sky"
* "An oil painting of a sailing ship in a storm, dramatic clouds, in the style of classical maritime art"
* "A flat vector illustration of a mountain hiker at sunrise, bold shapes, warm palette"

## Tips

* Describe the subject first, then the setting, then the lighting and style.
* Add a style word at the end: "photorealistic", "cinematic", "watercolor painting", "isometric illustration".
* If the result is close but not right, keep the prompt and change only the seed.
* If the result is washed out, try guidance 1.5 to 2.0 and 6 steps.
* Negative prompt ideas: "blurry, low quality, distorted, extra limbs, watermark, text".
