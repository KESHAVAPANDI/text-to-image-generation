The short version

Standard SDXL needs 50 or more steps. LCM-LoRA teaches it to take much larger steps, so the same model finishes in 4. That model needs a GPU, which this hosted page does not have, so generation stays in the local app.

1. Write a promptSubject first, then setting, then light and style.
2. Generate in 4 stepsLow guidance, around 1.0, is where LCM works best, in the local app.
3. Download the PNGSame prompt and seed gives the same image, every time.

How it works

Three steps, no account, no queue. The model loads once when the app starts, then every generation reuses it.

  1. Write a promptDescribe the subject, the setting, and the light. Add a negative prompt for things to avoid.
  2. Generate in 4 stepsThe LCM scheduler converges in 4 steps instead of 50. Keep the seed to reproduce a result, change it for a variation.
  3. Download the PNGImages are saved to static/generated_images/ and shown here with their seed, steps, and size.
