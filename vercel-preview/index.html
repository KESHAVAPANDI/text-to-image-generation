How it works

Three steps, no account, no queue. Each generation is a fresh request to the hosted model.

  1. Write a promptDescribe the subject, the setting, and the light. Flux follows the prompt literally, so concrete details help.
  2. GenerateThe page requests a new image for your exact prompt, size, model, and seed. A new seed gives a new variation.
  3. Download the PNGThe image is shown here with its model, seed, and size, and you can save it.
